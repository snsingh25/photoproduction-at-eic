# Photoproduction at EIC
We investigate the substructure of jets produced in photoproduction events at the proposed Electron-Ion Collider (EIC) with center-of-mass energies $\sqrt{s} = 30\text{--}140$ GeV.~The contributions from direct and resolved photoproduction sub-processes are analyzed separately. Events are generated using the PYTHIA8  Monte Carlo generator, and jets are reconstructed using the longitudinally invariant $k_T$ algorithm within the FastJet framework. We present a detailed study of the substructure of quark- and gluon-initiated jets, specifically making predictions for subjet multiplicities and integrated jet shapes. A clear distinction is observed between ``thick'' (gluon-initiated) and ``thin'' (quark-initiated) jets.~These results demonstrate the feasibility of distinguishing quark and gluon jets at the EIC and provide a baseline for future experimental selection cuts.
